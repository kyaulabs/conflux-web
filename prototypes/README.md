# CONFLUX listening room

Open `index.html` directly in a browser. The top navigation switches between three design studies. No server, build, account or network access is needed; external source links are optional.

| Page | Try this | What to compare |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signal Room (`index.html`) | Move cue position to bar 16, then take the crossfader from A to B. Watch the platter marker and both scopes. | A performer's first impression of the application. The signals are illustrations, not recorded tracks or audio-engine output. |
| The Setlist (`setlist.html`) | Select a milestone. Read its credits; each groove band represents one board issue, and a solid band means Done. | A light, editorial way to read the roadmap one milestone at a time. |
| Work Patchbay (`patchbay.html`) | Solo In Progress, then expand the routed issues. | A contributor's view of how work is distributed across milestones and Kanban lanes. |

The brief is three different experiences, not three skins: use the supplied CONFLUX identity, use D3 for the interactions and compare against a frozen snapshot. `assets/` and `tokens/` contain the supplied lockups, fonts, font licenses and brand stylesheet. D3 7.9.0 and its license are bundled in `vendor/` for offline review.

`snapshot.js` records 9 [milestones](https://github.com/kyaulabs/conflux/milestones) and 45 public repository issues from the [CONFLUX Roadmap](https://github.com/orgs/kyaulabs/projects/5) on 2026-11-23 UTC. Milestone open/closed labels come from the repository; Done, In Progress and Todo are board fields. Board issue totals do not imply feature or release qualification. Nothing writes back to GitHub.

These pages are isolated candidates for design selection, not a published site or playable browser port.
